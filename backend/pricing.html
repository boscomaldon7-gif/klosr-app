Common questions

Why is the free tier real (not a trial)?
Because solo founders closing their first 5 customers shouldn't be paying for a tool that's supposed to help them get to 5 customers. Klosr is free until you're making real money with it. When you upgrade to Pro, that's because Klosr is paying for itself 10x over — not because we forced you.
What counts as a "Vision analysis"?
Each time Klosr's screen-watcher sees a new sales-relevant frame and asks Claude Opus 4.7 to draft a response. The Free tier caps at 50/day (plenty for solo founders running 30-50 DMs). Pro is unlimited.
Do you store my screen captures?
No. Frames go directly to Anthropic for inference and are never stored on Klosr servers. Anthropic doesn't train on our API traffic. We log only event metadata (timestamp + intent + outcome) to power your pipeline analytics. Full privacy policy at /privacy.
Can I cancel?
Pro is monthly, no contract. Cancel from your account dashboard, takes effect at end of billing cycle. No "talk to sales" gate, no retention emails — if Klosr stops working for you, you stop paying.
What's coming on Pro that's not here yet?
Native Tauri desktop app (replaces the PWA, runs as menubar app, reads native WhatsApp Desktop / Slack Desktop without browser banner) — shipping ~4 weeks. Pro users get it included free. Roadmap on /changelog.
I'm a solo founder making $0 MRR. Will you really not charge me?
Correct. Use Klosr free until it's helped you close real revenue. Built by founders. We've been there.
